# LF1 Study Trackers

Interactive study trackers for the Charles University LF1 finals. Open one and use it in the browser. Your progress is saved privately on your own device and is never uploaded.

**LF1 Tracker, all years and subjects** — https://mhrdhs.github.io/Biochem-Tracker-LF1/hub/

Pick a year, then a subject. Third year currently has Pathology, Microbiology 2 and Propaedeutics in Internal Medicine.
